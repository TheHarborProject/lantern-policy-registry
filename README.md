<h1 align="center">Lantern Policy Registry</h1>

<p align="center">
  Versioned, reusable accessibility policies for the Lantern ecosystem.
</p>

This repository is the future home of shareable policy definitions for [Lantern](https://github.com/TheHarborProject/lantern). It will provide a central place to discover, version, and maintain reusable accessibility policy sets without coupling them to the Lantern CLI release cycle.

## Status

The registry is being initialized. Its policy format, validation rules, contribution workflow, and publishing process will be documented here as they are established.

Until those contracts are published, do not depend on this repository as a stable policy source.

## Related projects

- [Lantern](https://github.com/TheHarborProject/lantern) — accessibility auditing CLI and programmatic API.
- [Lantern TUI](https://github.com/TheHarborProject/lantern-tui) — interactive terminal interface for Lantern workflows.
